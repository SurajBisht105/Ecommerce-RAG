FAQ:
Q: Can I use third-party switches?
A: Yes, the hot-swap sockets support any Cherry MX compatible 3-pin or 5-pin switches from brands like Gateron, Kailh, and others.

Q: Does it work with Mac?
A: Yes, it includes Mac-specific keycaps and supports Mac key layouts. However, the configuration software is Windows-only.

Q: How loud are the Cherry MX Brown switches?
A: Cherry MX Brown switches produce a moderate sound level. They are quieter than Blue switches but louder than Silent Red switches. The pre-installed dampening foam helps reduce overall noise.

Q: Can I replace the keycaps?
A: Yes, the keyboard uses standard Cherry MX stems. Any MX-compatible keycap set will work.
